About Gardian

Pest-free gardening shouldn't require a chemistry degree or a bad back.

Gardian was born from a frustratingly familiar situation: a well-tended vegetable garden, a persistent squirrel population, and the realization that every existing "solution" asked you to choose between protecting your crops and actually accessing them.

After several seasons of iterating on a DIY fence-and-gate system — testing it against squirrels, chipmunks, raccoons, and the occasional toddler — we knew the core design was solid. What it needed was proper materials, real modularity, and a way for any gardener to configure it for their specific setup without needing a tape measure, a saw, and an afternoon to spare.

That's Gardian. A modular, reconfigurable enclosure system that snaps together, adapts to your beds, and never asks you to step over anything to harvest a tomato.

Ocean-Bound Plastic

What "ocean-bound" actually means

The term "ocean-bound plastic" refers to plastic waste collected in areas at high risk of ocean entry — typically within 50 kilometers of a coastline, in regions where formal waste management infrastructure is limited or absent.

Unlike post-consumer recycled content from developed-market collection systems, ocean-bound plastic requires active intervention: community-based collection programs, sorting, and certification. Gardian sources from suppliers with third-party certification of their ocean-bound plastic supply chains.

The finished material is a UV-stabilized, impact-resistant recycled plastic compound suitable for outdoor use. It does not require paint or surface treatments, will not splinter, and is resistant to the freeze-thaw cycling common in Zones 4–7.

Supply Chain
🌊

Plastic waste collected within 50km of at-risk coastlines by certified community programs in Southeast Asia and South America.

Sorted plastic processed into UV-stabilized compound pellets for injection molding. Freeze-thaw and impact resistance validated.
